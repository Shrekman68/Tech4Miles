A Little Bit About Me....

Hello Everyone! My name is Miles :)

I have always been interested in computers ever since I was but a young lad. My dad, who is a web designer introduce me to the art of computers at 4 years old once the game "Morrowind" came out and then I was hooked...

It was then I decided this is definitely something I would be into. It wasn't until after high school that I decided I needed to take the next big step. I enlisted into the United States Space Force to pursue a career in Cyber. This is where I would get my foot into the door and progress my career in Computer Science.

I started taking courses in college while in Active Duty and got out around March of 2025 to pursue my B.S. in Computer Science full-time. College courses weren't enough so I took a deep dive online and trips to the library to further enhance my knowledge of the subject.

Like I said before I am still quite new and still in the process of learning. Please bare with me as I progress, but if you have any questions or input that you would like to give me, please let me know!
